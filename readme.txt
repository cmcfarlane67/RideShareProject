At first, with only 20 cars for 50 passengers, a lot of people just never made it to their destinations. 
Cars would pass by full, go the wrong direction, or reach their own destination before dropping everyone off. 
It felt a bit chaotic, and I could see why having too few cars makes it really hard for everyone to get where they need to go.
When I doubled the number of cars to 40, things started to work a lot better. 
More passengers were able to hop on cars going in the right direction, and fewer got left behind. 
It was interesting to see how just adding more cars changed the whole system even though capacity and direction still mattered.

20 cars = 40% - 70% success rate
40 cars = 70%-90% success rate
